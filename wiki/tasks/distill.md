Read the complete assigned source unit, capture inventory/verdict, actual images/transcripts and resolved topic/schema. Produce the exact source-id/unit-id/topic candidate at the supplied default/custom distillation template; never write raw. Use type:compilation, artifact_role:topic_distill, source_ids, compiled_from direct raw support, actual authors and source-unit locators. source_id identifies originating work, not each chapter/file; retain supplied identity/lineage and never invent independent corroboration.

Distillation is structured detailed knowledge, not a shorter executive summary. Account for every in-scope explanation, claim, example, number, worked calculation, condition (including 'only after'), exception, qualification, uncertainty, definition, contradiction and material visual detail. Keep differences between source assertion and independently established truth explicit. Link each claim directly to its supporting raw span. Preserve alternative positions without false consensus; show inferential reasoning, unknowns and limits. Search related wiki content but use it only within authorized evidence/role restrictions. Include full meaning rather than topic keyword coverage. Emit declared coverage candidate mapping every expected unit exactly once with unit_id/topic/artifact_refs/disposition/reason/scope_rule; outside_scope must match the immutable declared scope rule; unavailable is not covered. Detail omitted for convenience is not outside_scope. No self-issued acceptance or post-generation review receipt.

Keep source facts and their interpretations in atomic, directly supported statements throughout the complete note, not only the main claims section. Separate unrelated metadata/absence assertions; do not combine several unknowns beneath one marker. Operational omission/correction receipts may name existing claim locations without repeating their factual contents. Preserve the full source meaning and required schema sections rather than shortening the declared scope to avoid annotation work.

The canonical confidence field describes evidence strength, not copying accuracy or source-text fidelity. Apply the selected schema and C-WIKI-SYNTH-4 in the supplied synthesis-quality checklist; a fidelity-only explanation cannot exempt frontmatter from that rule. A single-origin distillation must not claim high confidence merely because its quotes are exact. Keep its limited support explicit without inventing independent corroboration or moving factual narrative into Coverage/Verification Notes.
