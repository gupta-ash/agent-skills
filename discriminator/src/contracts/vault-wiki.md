ARTIFACT CLASS: vault-wiki

This review is of a compiled wiki page in a knowledge vault — not a capture of a source and not a
draft, but synthesis: someone read a pile of raw notes, worked out what they add up to, and wrote
the answer down so nobody has to read the raws again. Strategy and inference pages belong here
too, and so does compiled synthesis kept inside a project rather than in a vault.

WHO DEPENDS ON IT: the next reader — a person or a model — treats this page as settled and does
not go back underneath it. Fleet agents load these pages as context and act on them, and real
decisions get taken on what the page says, long after anyone remembers what the raws said.

WHAT "WRONG" MEANS FOR THIS CLASS: the page says something the material underneath it does not
support, and the reader has no way to notice.

GRADED CATEGORIES: truthfulness, usability
PRIMARY CATEGORY: truthfulness
NOT GRADED FOR THIS CLASS: stability, flexibility
FLOOR: a false claim that will drive a real decision is `critical`.

Truthfulness is primary because the page's only product is its claims. Nothing runs here and
nothing breaks; the page is simply believed. A page that is hard to follow costs the reader some
minutes — that is why usability is graded — but a wrong one costs whatever the reader does next.

THINGS THAT ARE DEFECTS HERE
  - A claim attributed to a named source that the source does not make, or makes only under
    conditions the page dropped.
  - A figure stated flatly — a number, date, price, dose, version, threshold — where the
    material behind it gave a range, an estimate, or a different value.
  - Somebody's opinion, forecast or recommendation restated as established fact, with the person
    and the hedging gone.
  - A citation, link or note reference that points at nothing, or at something that does not say
    what the page says it says.
  - Guidance that only held under circumstances the page never states, so a reader in different
    circumstances follows it wrongly.
  - Two passages that cannot both be true, leaving the reader to pick one.

THINGS THAT ARE NOT DEFECTS HERE
  - Synthesis that goes past any single raw note. That is the whole job. Challenge a conclusion
    only when it does not follow from what the page cites.
  - A subject the page does not cover. A compilation is allowed to be partial.
  - An uncited sentence of ordinary background, as opposed to an uncited specific claim.
  - How the page is titled, tagged, filed, ordered or laid out.
